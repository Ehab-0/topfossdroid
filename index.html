About rankings and data

Popular combines repository stars, measured F-Droid downloads, and recent activity.

Most Downloaded uses available F-Droid download metrics. These are not total Android installs.

Most Starred uses public GitHub, GitLab, and Codeberg repository stars.

Trending uses stars gained over the last 30 days when sufficient history exists.

Fresh & Popular shows Popular apps updated within 180 days with at least 500 stars or 10,000 measured downloads.

Hidden Gems shows apps updated within one year, below 5,000 stars, and with at least 25,000 measured downloads.

Discovery comes from F-Droid-compatible catalogs and official project repositories. Archive and nightly-only apps are excluded from current rankings. Forge metadata and F-Droid download metrics enrich the catalog; source trust is not a ranking signal.

Stars measure repository popularity, not app quality.
